## Long description (no URLs)

Concrete Contractors Estimator does the take-off that concrete contractors do before every pour, right on your phone.

ESTIMATE
- Slabs, driveways, patios, garage floors and pool decks from length, width and thickness
- Continuous footings, round columns and sonotubes, and solid steps
- Cubic feet and cubic yards with a waste allowance you set, plus the quantity to order from a ready-mix plant, rounded up to the next quarter yard
- How many 80, 60 or 40 lb bags that is, and whether bags or a ready-mix truck make more sense
- Rebar grid for any spacing (bars each way, linear feet and 20 ft sticks with lap), or the number of welded-wire mesh sheets
- A rough installed cost range for broom, colored, exposed-aggregate or stamped finishes, including thicker slabs and tear-out of old concrete

POUR DAY PLANNER
- Enter the forecast high and the month to get a heat-risk level and the best start window
- Practical hot-weather tips: pre-wet the subgrade, keep the mix cool, set retarder, shade and windbreaks, splitting big pours
- Monsoon-season reminders from June to September
- A cure schedule: foot traffic, vehicles, moist curing and full strength

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
- No account, no ads, no tracking
- No permissions; nothing you type leaves your phone

The app is published by Phoenix Concrete Contractors, a licensed Arizona concrete contractor for driveways, patios, stamped concrete, pool decks and repairs. Cost figures are planning ranges, not a quote.

## Testing instructions for Amazon reviewers

No login required. Open the app: the Estimate tab shows a 20 x 10 ft, 4 in slab = 2.72 cubic yards and 123 x 80 lb bags.

forecast slider. The About tab has links to the publisher's website, the privacy policy and the source code.
