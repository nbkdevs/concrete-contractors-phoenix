# Privacy Policy

Concrete Contractors Estimator (Android app, packages, web page) and the Slab & Bag Calculator browser
extension are published by Phoenix Concrete Contractors. Our website is
[Concrete Contractors Phoenix](https://concretecontractorsphoenix.net/), and its own privacy policy is at
https://concretecontractorsphoenix.net/privacy-policy/.

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

## What we collect

Nothing. The calculators run entirely on your device. They do not ask for an account, do not use analytics,
advertising or crash-reporting SDKs, do not read your location, contacts or files, and do not send the numbers
you type anywhere. Nothing is stored after you close the app, page or popup.

## Permissions

- Android app: requests no permissions and does not use the internet.
  Tapping a link (our website, this policy, the source code) opens it in your browser.
- Browser extension: no permissions at all (`"permissions": []`), no host access, no remote code.

## Third parties

None. If you open our website or GitHub from the app, those sites' own policies apply.

## Children

The tools are general-purpose calculators and collect no data from anyone, including children.

## Contact

Questions: use the contact options on https://concretecontractorsphoenix.net/ or open an issue at
https://github.com/nbkdevs/concrete-contractors-phoenix/issues.
